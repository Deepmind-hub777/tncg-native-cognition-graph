# 06 — Pilot Studies

This section contains protocols, instruments, analysis plans, and reports for empirical pilots.

## Pilot 01 — Grade 5–8 trilingual inquiry

Working theme: **How do different cultures understand doing good?**

Initial story set:
- Chinese: *刻舟求剑* — change, fixation, and situational judgment
- English / Biblical tradition: *The Good Samaritan* — compassion, neighbor, and action
- Thai Buddhist cultural tradition: *พระมาลัย* or a carefully sourced Jataka story, with historical and regional context stated explicitly

The pilot examines how learners interpret concepts and compare narratives. It must not assume that one story represents an entire culture or that religious traditions are interchangeable.

Evaluation may include cultural understanding, semantic distinction, reasoning, AI literacy, collaboration, and expression. Publish the protocol and limitations before making effectiveness claims.

No identifiable student data may be committed to this public repository.
