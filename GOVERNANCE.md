# TNCG Project Governance

## Purpose and status

This document describes how the open TNCG project manages research claims, contributions, revisions, and learner protection. TNCG is an evolving research framework; publication here does not imply academic consensus, validation, or institutional endorsement.

## Decision principles

- Evidence before authority or popularity
- Clear separation of data, interpretation, and hypothesis
- Transparent revision history
- Reproducible methods where practical
- Respect for linguistic and cultural plurality
- Safety, privacy, and informed participation

## Research integrity

Cite primary or authoritative sources where possible, identify uncertainty, and document methodological limitations. Preserve conflicting interpretations when evidence does not resolve them.

## Cultural and linguistic review

Claims about language use or cultural practice should be reviewed by competent speakers or relevant subject specialists where feasible. No contributor should be treated as the definitive representative of a language or culture.

## Learner data and safeguarding

Do not publish identifiable learner data, private conversations, student names, contact details, or sensitive records. Research involving minors requires appropriate permissions, age-appropriate assent, data minimization, and compliance with applicable laws and school policies. Anonymization alone may not eliminate re-identification risk.

## Licensing

Repository documentation is generally offered under CC BY 4.0 unless a file says otherwise. Third-party materials retain their original rights and terms. Software code must carry a separate, clearly stated software license before redistribution.

## Changes

Governance changes should be proposed transparently through an issue or pull request, with rationale and a record of the decision.
