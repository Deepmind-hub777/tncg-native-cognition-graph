# TNCG — Trilingual Native Cognition Graph

**三语原生认知图谱**

> From language alignment to cognitive interoperability — building an AI-native learning infrastructure that respects the native semantic worlds of Chinese, Thai, and English.

## What is TNCG?

TNCG is an evolving research and development framework for representing, comparing, and teaching concepts across Chinese, Thai, and English without reducing their distinct semantic, pragmatic, and cultural contexts to direct translation.

It investigates how learners understand, distinguish, reason with, express, and transfer concepts across languages.

**Core research question**

> Can a learner think across three languages without losing the native conceptual world of each language?

## Five connected areas

1. **Cognitive theory** — multilingual conceptual representation, cross-linguistic influence, and translanguaging.
2. **Native semantic graphs** — language-specific concepts, senses, usage, cultural contexts, and relations.
3. **AI reasoning** — graph-based retrieval, word-sense disambiguation, and learner-aware reasoning.
4. **Pedagogy** — inquiry, project-based learning, Socratic dialogue, and curriculum mapping.
5. **Learning intelligence** — misconceptions, learning trajectories, and cross-language transfer measurement.

## Research principles

- Preserve language-specific meanings; do not assume one-to-one equivalence.
- Distinguish evidence, interpretation, and hypotheses.
- Avoid treating any language or culture as internally uniform.
- Make claims testable and document limitations.
- Use AI as a learning aid, not as a substitute for learner reasoning.
- Protect learner privacy and obtain appropriate consent for research data.

TNCG is an emerging research framework, not an established or universally accepted theory. Its definitions and methods are expected to evolve through review and empirical testing.

## Project status

The learning library contains trilingual story materials, adult Thai speaking workshops, and an interactive Qianziwen learning module. The Qianziwen module includes a 250-group, 63-page draft corpus; its remaining pinyin, explanations, English notes, images, audio, and stroke-order data retain their review statuses. See [`09-learning-library/qianziwen/README.md`](09-learning-library/qianziwen/README.md) for the detailed status and local run instructions.
