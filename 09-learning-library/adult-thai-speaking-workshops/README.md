# Adult Thai Speaking Workshops

This collection records adult TNCG Thai speaking workshops and practical role-play sessions.

## File convention

`YYYY-MM-DD-session-NN-topic-level.md`

Where appropriate, include:
- Session objective and CEFR / Thai proficiency target
- Real-life scenario and role cards
- Dialogue in Thai with natural English and Chinese support
- Useful vocabulary, particles, politeness, and sentence patterns
- Learner practice prompts
- Corrections with brief explanations
- Natural alternatives and register notes
- Pronunciation or tone reminders
- A short review task and next-session bridge

## Privacy

Do not publish raw personal speech recordings, names, contact details, or sensitive personal stories. Use anonymized or generalized examples. Preserve the language-learning substance without exposing private learner information.
